## What SanctionBench adds

SanctionBench is intentionally narrower than these suites. Its contribution is an auditable benchmark for a single professionally catastrophic failure mode:

- organic positives come from authority failures that a court found or implied in a real proceeding;
- every released positive carries a database-row → ruling → page/excerpt → independent lookup chain;
- real-but-obscure matched distractors make false accusations measurable rather than allowing a flag-everything strategy;
- closed-book and standardized CourtListener-assisted conditions isolate calibrated skepticism from evidence-backed verification;
- source-decision and first-observed dates, plus a separate memorization track, make contamination an explicit analysis dimension; and
- public labels are described as development gold, not a blind leaderboard. A defensible leaderboard requires a later private, time-forward test set.

No claim is made that one benchmark's legal task coverage, source corpus, or scoring is interchangeable with another's. The comparison above is structural and based only on artifacts visible at the pinned commits.
