# Code of conduct

Contributors must communicate respectfully, focus criticism on the work, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or publication of private information. Benchmark discussions can concern named lawyers, litigants, and judicial sanctions; use neutral, evidence-based language and do not turn dataset records into personal attacks.

Report sensitive conduct issues through the published repository's private reporting channel. If no private channel is available, request one publicly without describing the incident or identifying the affected person. Maintainers may remove abusive content and restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and constructive.
